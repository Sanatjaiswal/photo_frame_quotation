Before we price it

Which countries can actually be served

The brief mentions shipping to Pakistan. That lane needs to be addressed honestly up front, because it changes what the website can promise a customer at checkout.

Requires a decision

India → Pakistan is not commercially shippable today

India suspended bilateral trade with Pakistan in 2019 and postal exchange between the two countries has been suspended since. No mainstream courier operating in India — DHL, FedEx, Aramex, Delhivery, DTDC, India Post — accepts retail consignments to Pakistan. A website that takes a Pakistani order and payment will end up refunding it.

How we handle it: the country list is a table in the admin panel, not hard-coded. Pakistan sits in it as a disabled row from day one. If the route ever reopens, the shop switches it on and sets a price — no development work, no invoice from us. Nothing is lost by leaving it off at launch, and a lot of credibility is lost by leaving it on.

UK, USA, Canada, Australia, Singapore, Malaysia. Highest-value gifting orders and the strongest reason to build multi-currency at all.

Disabled row

Pakistan

Configured in the system, switched off at checkout. Ready to enable if the route reopens.

Export paperwork the shop will need

An IEC (Importer Exporter Code) from DGFT, free to obtain, plus a commercial invoice and packing slip per international parcel. Under the CSB-V courier route, consignments below ₹5,00,000 clear on a simplified declaration. We can generate these documents automatically — see the automation menu.

Taking money from abroad

Razorpay International needs separate activation and settles at roughly 3% + GST versus ~2% domestic. PayPal works as a fallback for the NRI corridor. Whichever is chosen, the shop holds the merchant account — we integrate it, we never handle the funds.

Pricing per country, not one converted price

Every country gets its own price row, not a live FX conversion of the Indian price. That lets the shop bury shipping and duty into a Dubai price without discounting India, which is how gifting stores in this category actually make margin.
